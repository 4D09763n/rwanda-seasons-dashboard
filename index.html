Quick answers

Answers to the main questions addressed by this project.

Which season generally has the most rainfall?

The long rainy season (March to May) has the highest average monthly rainfall. The single wettest month in the 1991-2020 data is November.

When are thunderstorms more common?

They are associated with rainy periods, especially the long and short rainy seasons. Frequency varies by place and year.

When can weather-related disasters occur?

Flooding and landslides can occur during intense rainfall in either rainy season. Local conditions matter.

Weather-related hazards

Heavy rainfall can contribute to several hazards.

🌊

Flooding

Intense rain can cause water to accumulate or overflow, especially in low-lying and poorly drained areas.

⛰️

Landslides

Prolonged or heavy rainfall can increase slope instability, particularly on steep or vulnerable land.

⚡

Thunderstorms & lightning

Storms may bring lightning, heavy downpours and strong winds. Follow official warnings.

Practical safety reminders

  • Pay attention to official weather alerts and local instructions.
  • During thunderstorms, move indoors and stay away from open areas and isolated tall objects.
  • Do not walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Avoid steep slopes and areas showing signs of ground movement after heavy rain.
  • In an emergency, follow guidance from local authorities and emergency services.
